# Saga Forge

## Register
product

## Users
A group of friends writing themselves into films, series, books and other fictional universes. They manage different versions of themselves and canon characters in multiple independent stories.

## Product Purpose
Read multi-part illustrated stories and manage their characters, scenes, media and continuity using existing local APIs.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, immersive, clear. The owner requested large imagery and a redesigned color system while retaining comfortable reading.

## Anti-references
Avoid crowded menus, tiny reading text, persistent glow, horizontal-only bookshelves and Game of Thrones-specific defaults for unrelated universes.

## Design Principles
- Organize by universe, then story, then the cast in that story.
- Keep self/friend characters distinct from canon characters.
- Reading is the primary library action.
- Keep writing and media tools available without crowding the story.
- Use consistent Thai labels and predictable navigation.
- Fit small screens without losing functionality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable Thai text, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion and comfortable touch targets. WCAG AA contrast is a design target, not a certification.
